# Signal Generation

---

# 1. Purpose

## Overview

The Signal Generation architecture defines the standardized framework for producing investment signals from evaluated market opportunities.

Signals represent structured business observations generated by investment strategies after evaluating candidate securities.

Signals communicate investment intent.

They do not authorize trading.

---

# Objectives

The Signal Generation architecture exists to:

- standardize signal generation
- separate signals from Recommendations
- support reusable signal models
- preserve deterministic behaviour
- simplify downstream processing
- maintain traceability
- support future extensibility

---

# Scope

This specification defines:

- signal architecture
- signal lifecycle
- signal classification
- signal confidence
- signal outputs
- platform relationships
- architectural extension

This specification does not define:

- investment discovery
- recommendation generation
- portfolio allocation
- trade execution
- risk evaluation

These responsibilities are defined in their respective architectural specifications.

---

# Position within the Platform Architecture

Signal Generation operates after Strategy Evaluation.

The conceptual architecture is:

```text
Discovery
        │
        ▼
Strategy Evaluation
        │
        ▼
Signal Generation
        │
        ▼
Recommendation Engine
```

Signal Generation converts business evaluation into standardized investment signals.

---

# Architectural Responsibility

Signal Generation is responsible for:

- generating investment signals
- classifying signals
- assigning confidence
- publishing signal events
- recording signal history
- producing standardized signal artifacts

Signal Generation is not responsible for:

- generating Recommendations
- evaluating execution risk
- executing Orders
- managing Portfolios
- communicating with brokers

Signals communicate business intent.

Recommendations communicate business actions.

---

# Platform Relationships

Within the Platform Architecture, Signal Generation consists of:

Configuration

- Signal Policies

Registry

- Signal Registry

Business Engine

- Signal Engine

Run

- Signal Run

Artifact

- Signal
- Signal Result

Event

- Signal Events

Operational Control

- Signal Controls

The architecture follows the standardized Platform Architecture patterns.

---

# Guiding Principles

Signal Generation follows these principles:

- deterministic signal generation
- business transparency
- modular architecture
- technology independence
- complete traceability
- reusable signal models
- operational consistency

---

# Success Criteria

A successful Signal Generation implementation should ensure that:

- identical evaluations produce identical signals
- signals remain independent of Recommendations
- signal history is preserved
- downstream systems receive standardized signals
- operational visibility is complete
- signal generation remains explainable

The architecture described in this specification establishes the standardized signal generation framework for every investment strategy within the StoX Platform.

# 2. Signal Philosophy

## Overview

The Signal Philosophy establishes the principles governing how investment signals are produced within the StoX Platform.

Signals represent structured business conclusions derived from strategy evaluation.

Signals describe observations.

They do not determine execution.

---

# Signal as a Business Capability

Signal Generation is responsible for translating evaluated business conditions into standardized investment signals.

Typical responsibilities include:

- interpreting strategy outcomes
- producing business signals
- assigning classifications
- attaching confidence
- preserving rationale

Signals communicate investment observations.

---

# Separation of Responsibilities

Business responsibilities are divided across architectural layers.

Strategy Evaluation

Responsible for:

- evaluating investment methodology
- producing business outcomes

Signal Generation

Responsible for:

- converting outcomes into standardized signals

Recommendation Engine

Responsible for:

- converting signals into Recommendations

Each layer contributes one business responsibility.

---

# Deterministic Signal Generation

Signal Generation shall remain deterministic.

Given identical:

- Strategy Output
- configuration
- execution context
- business rules

the generated signals shall always be identical.

Signal generation shall not depend upon hidden operational state.

---

# Explainability

Every signal should be explainable.

Operators should understand:

- why the signal was generated
- which business rules contributed
- confidence assigned
- resulting classification

Signal Generation shall remain transparent.

---

# Reusability

Signals should be reusable across:

- recommendation engines
- portfolio simulations
- analytics
- reporting
- paper trading
- live trading

Signals should represent reusable business artifacts.

---

# Technology Independence

The Signal Generation architecture defines business concepts.

It does not depend upon:

- programming language
- analytics engine
- rule engine
- infrastructure technology
- storage technology

Technology remains an implementation decision.

---

# Design Principles

The Signal Philosophy shall:

- remain deterministic
- remain explainable
- remain reusable
- preserve business separation
- remain technology-independent
- support complete traceability

Signals communicate business observations.

Recommendations communicate business actions.

---

# Summary

The Signal Philosophy establishes a deterministic, reusable and technology-independent foundation for producing investment signals within the StoX Platform.

By separating signal generation from both strategy evaluation and recommendation generation while preserving transparency and complete traceability, the platform enables standardized downstream investment processing.

# 4. Signal Lifecycle

## Overview

The Signal Lifecycle defines the standardized operational stages followed by every investment signal from creation until retirement.

Every signal progresses through deterministic lifecycle stages while preserving complete traceability and operational consistency.

The Signal Lifecycle governs signals.

It does not govern Recommendations.

---

# Purpose

The Signal Lifecycle exists to:

- standardize signal evolution
- preserve operational consistency
- support downstream processing
- maintain signal history
- simplify lifecycle management
- support auditing

Every signal shall follow the same lifecycle.

---

# Lifecycle Model

The conceptual Signal Lifecycle is:

```text
Generated
        │
        ▼
Validated
        │
        ▼
Published
        │
        ▼
Consumed
        │
        ▼
Expired
```

Alternative terminal states may include:

- Withdrawn
- Cancelled

Every signal shall terminate in exactly one final state.

---

# Generated

Signal generation begins immediately after Strategy Evaluation completes.

Typical activities include:

- interpret Strategy Output
- determine signal type
- assign identifier
- initialize metadata

Generation establishes the initial signal.

---

# Validated

Validation verifies signal completeness and consistency.

Typical validation includes:

- mandatory attributes present
- originating strategy available
- confidence assigned
- classification completed
- policy compliance

Only validated signals shall be published.

---

# Published

Publication makes the signal available to downstream platform capabilities.

Publication includes:

- registry update
- event publication
- metadata preservation
- availability notification

Published signals become eligible for downstream consumption.

---

# Consumed

Signals may be consumed by downstream platform capabilities.

Typical consumers include:

- Recommendation Engine
- Analytics
- Reporting
- Paper Trading
- Live Trading

Consumption does not modify the original signal.

---

# Expired

Signals may expire after their business relevance has ended.

Typical reasons include:

- time validity exceeded
- market conditions changed
- newer signal available
- originating strategy updated

Expired signals remain historically available.

---

# Withdrawn

A signal may be withdrawn before normal expiration.

Typical reasons include:

- business rule invalidated
- strategy correction
- data correction
- operational cancellation

Withdrawn signals shall remain historically traceable.

---

# Lifecycle Traceability

Every Signal Lifecycle shall record:

- signal identifier
- originating strategy
- lifecycle states
- transition timestamps
- publication history
- final status

Lifecycle history supports auditing and operational analysis.

---

# Design Principles

The Signal Lifecycle shall:

- remain deterministic
- preserve signal history
- support downstream processing
- remain technology-independent
- support complete traceability
- maintain lifecycle consistency

The Signal Lifecycle governs business observations.

Recommendation Lifecycle governs business actions.

---

# Summary

The Signal Lifecycle provides the standardized operational model governing every investment signal within the StoX Platform.

By defining deterministic lifecycle stages while preserving complete history and operational transparency, the platform enables reliable downstream processing and long-term signal governance.

Signal Types communicate business intent.

```text
Signal Classification
        │
        ▼
Signal Type
        │
        ▼

```

Classification transforms business outcomes into standardized business observations.

---

# Directional Signals

Directional Signals communicate market direction.

Typical examples include:

- Bullish
- Bearish
- Neutral

Directional signals describe business expectation.

They do not imply trading decisions.

---

# Entry Signals

Entry Signals indicate that predefined business conditions for entering a position have been satisfied.

Examples include:

- Entry Opportunity
- Breakout Detected
- Trend Confirmation
- Pullback Entry

Entry Signals communicate opportunity.

Exit Signals indicate that predefined business conditions for exiting a position have been satisfied.

Examples include:

- Exit Opportunity
- Stop Condition
- Target Achieved
- Trend Reversal

Exit Signals communicate business observations.

They do not initiate order execution.

# Informational Signals

Informational Signals communicate business information that may influence downstream decision making.

Examples include:

- High Volatility
- Low Liquidity
- Earnings Event
- Corporate Action
- Market Regime Change

Informational signals provide context rather than direction.

---

# Composite Signals

More advanced strategies may combine multiple business observations into Composite Signals.

Conceptually:

```text
Trend Signal

        │
        ├── Volume Signal
        │
        └── Risk Signal
```

Composite Signals provide richer business context while preserving the originating observations.

---

# Signal Independence

Signal Types shall remain independent.

A Signal Type should not:

- modify another signal
- override another classification
- depend upon internal implementation of another signal

Signals may coexist while remaining logically independent.

---

# Signal Consistency

Every Signal Type should define:

- business meaning
- originating conditions
- downstream interpretation
- lifecycle behaviour
- applicable markets

Signal definitions shall remain standardized across the platform.

---

# Design Principles

Signal Types shall:

- remain business-oriented
- remain standardized
- preserve business separation
- support interoperability
- remain technology-independent
- support extensibility

Signal Types classify business observations.

They do not determine business actions.

---

# Summary

Signal Types provide a standardized business classification for investment observations produced by the Strategy Engine.

By separating business observations into well-defined signal categories while preserving architectural independence and business consistency, the platform enables reusable and interoperable downstream processing.

# 9. Extension Model

## Overview

The Signal Generation architecture is designed to evolve through disciplined extension rather than architectural redesign.

Future signal capabilities should extend existing signal concepts while preserving deterministic behaviour, standardized signal representation and architectural separation.

The objective is to improve business expressiveness without increasing architectural complexity.

---

# Extension Philosophy

The Signal Generation architecture should evolve using the following order of preference.

```text
Reuse Existing Signal Type

↓

Extend Existing Signal Metadata

↓

Extend Signal Classification

↓

Extend Signal Components

↓

Introduce New Architectural Component (Exceptional)
```

Existing architectural abstractions should always be reused wherever practical.

---

# Extending Signal Types

Future platform versions may introduce additional business signal classifications.

Examples include:

- sentiment signals
- macroeconomic signals
- event-driven signals
- sector rotation signals
- AI-assisted signals
- cross-market signals

---

# Extending Confidence

Future confidence capabilities may include:

- probabilistic confidence
- weighted confidence
- multi-factor confidence
- adaptive confidence
- AI-assisted confidence assessment

Confidence enhancements shall preserve explainability wherever practical.

---

# Extending Signal Processing

- streaming signal generation

Signal processing enhancements shall preserve deterministic signal production.

# Extending Operational Capabilities

# AI-Assisted Signal Generation

Future AI capabilities may assist Signal Generation by providing:

- signal summarization
- business explanation generation

# Backward Compatibility

Signal evolution should preserve compatibility wherever practical.

Existing:

- Signal Types
- Signal artifacts

- Signal Results
- Signal Events

should remain valid after architectural enhancements.

# Architectural Review

Every significant Signal Generation enhancement should be reviewed to ensure that it:

- preserves deterministic behaviour
- supports business explainability
- preserves architectural boundaries
- remains technology-independent
- supports operational scalability
- aligns with Platform Architecture principles

# Design Principles

Signal Generation extensions shall:

- remain deterministic
- preserve business separation
- support complete traceability
- favour extension over redesign
- remain technology-independent
- support operational scalability

Signal Generation should evolve without changing the responsibilities of Recommendation generation.

---

# Summary

The Signal Generation architecture is designed to evolve through disciplined extension while preserving standardized business observations, reusable signal models and deterministic signal generation.
