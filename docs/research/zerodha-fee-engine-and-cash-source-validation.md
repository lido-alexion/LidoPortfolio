# Zerodha fee engine and source cash validation

This implementation adds a deterministic whole-trade-date fee calculator while preserving existing stored transaction fees. It does not recalculate or rewrite historical transaction rows.

## Fee model

`FeeCalculatorService::calculateGrouped()` and `calculateGroupedTradeDateFees()` accept a complete batch, group daily statutory charges at invoice precision, then allocate each invoice amount to rows by turnover using a stable largest-remainder cent allocation. STT is rounded once across the delivery day; stamp duty is rounded once on the buy side across venues; transaction charges remain separated by side and execution venue; SEBI charge is day-aggregated; GST is calculated on taxable charges and allocated back to rows. DP remains on the separate ISIN/day sell allocation path and is deliberately omitted from contract-note fee comparisons.

The ordinary single-trade methods remain available for a trade entered without its complete same-day batch. `TransactionWriteService` supplies the trade date to the PHP authoritative calculation, and the JavaScript calculator accepts the same date. The caller must provide the full batch to use invoice-level grouped allocation. Existing rows are deliberately not redistributed because changing their fee fields also changes already-posted cash and realized P/L; grouped recalculation across such rows needs an audited cash-ledger correction path.

Zerodha currently publishes NSE equity delivery transaction charges of 0.00307% to clients. Zerodha's published explanation for the October 2024 change gives the exchange component as 0.00297%; its current client rate includes the ₹0.10/lakh NSE IPFT component. Before October 2024, NSE used progressive monthly turnover slabs, so a single historical rate cannot be proven from the transaction alone. The engine uses the published ₹3.22/lakh base slab as its pre-change estimate; contract-note rates should override it when their monthly rate is known. [Zerodha charges](https://www.zerodha.com/charges), [Zerodha October 2024 rate notice](https://zerodha.com/marketintel/bulletin/391488/revision-in-transactions-charges-from-1st-october-2024), [NSE March 2024 slab circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/FA61137.pdf).

The grouped engine is still an approximation for charges whose source statement does not expose every matching order/settlement attribute. It makes invoice totals reconcile at day level and allocates deterministically; the allocation is not proof of a specific fill's broker-assessed fee. Brokerage contract-note rounding, settlement variants, and source-reported trade quantities/prices can leave small residuals. Zerodha's published equity delivery rates and DP charge are documented in its [charges schedule](https://www.zerodha.com/charges); STT is 0.1% on both delivery sides and is rounded to the nearest rupee per the [Zerodha STT explanation](https://support.zerodha.com/category/account-opening/resident-individual/ri-charges/articles/how-is-the-securities-transaction-tax-stt-calculated).

## Private-source qualification performed

The private 46-note/89-transaction evidence was read locally only. No PDFs, ledger exports, symbols, order identifiers, personal identifiers, or per-transaction private rows are included in this repository. On the 17 source days marked comparable by exact grouped quantity and gross, fee total absolute daily error went from ₹8.44 under the row calculator to ₹0.21 under day grouping. Component absolute error totals changed as follows: STT ₹4.00→₹0.00, stamp ₹4.46→₹0.00, transaction charges ₹0.02→₹0.00, SEBI ₹0.01→₹0.00, and GST ₹0.10→₹0.08. The grouped total differs by at most ₹0.02 on any of these days; the remaining paise-scale variance is consistent with the tiny contract-note brokerage rounding line and invoice line rounding. The totals across those 17 days were ₹3,366.16 current calculator, ₹3,363.26 note charges, and ₹3,363.05 grouped estimate. This is a source-backed aggregate qualification, not a claim that every individual transaction allocation is exact.

Anonymized tests exercise the June 19 pattern (three buy fills: note STT ₹249 and stamp ₹37 while per-fill rounding yields a higher total), and an October 7 mixed NSE/BSE buy plus sell pattern (note STT ₹278 and stamp ₹36). The grouped tests check a meaningful before/after reduction across both fixture days and verify that allocated row cents sum to the grouped invoice.

## Read-only cash evidence command

`php artisan cash:reconcile-source /secure/path/evidence.json --output=/secure/path/report.json` accepts normalized profile-scoped JSON with `profile_id`, `transactions`, `bank_flows`, optional `account_charges` (AMC/DDPI/service_fee), optional `broker_flows`, and an optional `closing_balance_snapshot`. It reads only the supplied file, does not query or mutate the database, and has no apply option. Every trade cash delta is `buy = -(quantity × price + stored fees)` or `sell = quantity × price - stored fees`. Stored fees include a DP allocation. DP broker rows are reported separately and are never added again to the trade cash total. A broker trade link must point to one unique supplied transaction and match its calculated cash effect. Cross-profile data, duplicate links, and unmatched trade cash fail closed.

When a closing snapshot is supplied, the report derives at most one opening residual from that snapshot less the supplied trade and bank flows. The report labels it `derived_opening_balance`, records the snapshot provenance, and explicitly says it is not an observed deposit. An omitted or off-StoX trade history remains part of that residual; it must not be presented as cash funding or used to claim broker closing-balance agreement.

Read-only source review identified profile 1 adjustment row 52 as a ₹7,228,613.4240 synthetic reconstruction opening offset, not a bank deposit. Tiny normalization/reconciliation rows were IDs 244, 245, 248, 252, and 253, with signed amounts +₹0.0001, −₹0.0022, +₹0.0015, −₹0.0008, and −₹0.0001. No live rows were changed. A future cleanup should be a separately reviewed, reversible append-only plan: verify each row's current exact source ID, effective date, account, and downstream balance; append a linked reversal for each approved synthetic entry in reverse chronological order; derive cash from the remaining source movements and retain at most one clearly labelled opening residual. Do not delete or mutate source entries, do not remove the independently sourced AMC/DDPI adjustments, and do not treat the residual as a deposit. The source CSV also includes off-StoX broker activity, so it cannot establish StoX-only cash closing balance.
