# Kite daily CNC trade sync and reconciliation

StoX imports executed NSE/BSE equity trades with Kite product `CNC`. MIS, NRML, MTF, derivatives, and other products are excluded. Each fill is written through `TransactionWriteService`, so the configured transaction fee engine, holdings calculation, realizations and cash ledger are applied. The unique profile/trade-ID key makes repeated retrieval idempotent. Trade ID, order ID, execution timestamp, exchange, product and broker evidence remain in `kite_cnc_trade_imports`.

Kite's Trades API supplies the current trading day's tradebook. The scheduled sync runs after the configured market close plus reconciliation delay (30 minutes by default), and reconnect performs current-day catch-up immediately. The manual Sync CNC trades action is also available. A later session is not represented as successfully recovered when the broker API can no longer retrieve it: a gap is persisted and exposed for review.

Existing transactions are matched by stock, side, trade date, aggregate quantity and weighted execution price. An unambiguous match links the Kite executions to the existing row; ambiguous or non-exact candidates are retained in `manual_match_review` and are not imported a second time. Missing instrument mappings and invalid CNC rows are reported in sync counts. Sell fills that cannot be safely attributed to an owner are not guessed.

Reconciliation compares the current StoX ledger with Kite holdings. Kite `quantity`, `t1_quantity` and `used_quantity` are normalized. Recent imported or matched CNC executions are grouped across unsettled equity sessions, using the trading calendar. A settlement adjustment is applied only if its complete net quantity exactly explains the StoX/Kite difference; partial or ambiguous reflection stays a discrepancy. Broker positions are retained as raw evidence and are not added to holdings, because they include MIS/NRML and other non-delivery products. Missing holdings, positions, funds or tradebook evidence fails the run instead of producing a clean comparison.

The holdings/funds reconciliation runs after trade sync near 16:00 IST and can be triggered manually. Live execution also requires one successful holdings/funds baseline per equity session. Before every BUY, StoX obtains fresh Kite buying power and subtracts local pending buy reservations and estimated fees. Before every SELL, it obtains current sellable holdings and subtracts pending sell quantities. Unknown/partial broker order states prevent another order until reconciled. Confirmed fill deltas are recorded once; full portfolio reconciliation does not run after each order fill.

The current Kite funds endpoint reports available equity buying power, not a historical or complete settlement ledger. Cash differences remain visible; this change does not create cash adjustments or new funds snapshots.
